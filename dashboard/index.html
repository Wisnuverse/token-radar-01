TOKEN LAUNCH RADAR

Protocols that record no token, ranked by what a launch would be worth — priced off the snapshot's own live tokens. Snapshot: DefiLlama protocols, as of .

How the value is priced

implied = TVL × blended × growth × maturity × chain

blended is the geometric mean of two multiples fitted from the snapshot's own live tokens: the category multiple (median mcap/TVL of the live tokens in the category, shrunk toward the global median with k = 5, clamped to the live p10–p90 band) and the size-curve multiple (a log-log mcap-on-TVL regression). A category with fewer than 3 traded peers does not price itself: it falls back to the snapshot-wide median, and the detail view says so. Each row also carries a value band — the point estimate ÷ and × one median leave-one-out error — because a point without its uncertainty is a claim the model cannot support. Every farm plan is netted at the band's low end — the farmer's expected gross (10% of launch value, pro-rata by capital) minus the plan's own gas — with the break-even capital beside it. The set-aside rows carry demoted values priced off their size neighbours, never ranked.

Validation (leave-one-out): Audit: A protocol is said to record no token, never to have none: its DefiLlama record may simply not carry one.
