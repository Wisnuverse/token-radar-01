Protocols that record no token, ranked by what a launch would be worth — priced off the snapshot's own live tokens. Snapshot: DefiLlama protocols, as of .

How the value is priced

implied = TVL × blended × growth × maturity × chain

blended is the geometric mean of two multiples fitted from the snapshot's own live tokens: the category multiple (median mcap/TVL of the live tokens in the category, shrunk toward the global median with k = 5, clamped to the live p10–p90 band) and the size-curve multiple (a log-log mcap-on-TVL regression). The size curve is weak — its R² is shown below — so it is one blended signal, never the value on its own. growth is 1 + clamped 7-day TVL change × 0.5. maturity rewards protocols that ran longer with no token. chain is a small multi-chain durability signal (1.00–1.20). No protocol name is ever special-cased.
